# Arduino Starter Kits

This page lists projects and resources for the Arduino Super Starter Kit and the Mega 2560 Kit used in our computer science circle.

## LA036 Super Starter Kit for Arduino UNO

This kit includes an Arduino Uno (CH340) and various components for learning the basics of electronics and programming.

### Projects Included

1. **Project 1: Hello World** - Basic serial communication.
2. **Project 2: LED Blinking** - The first step in controlling hardware.
3. **Project 3: RGB LED** - Mixing colors with an RGB LED.
4. **Project 4: Digital Inputs** - Using push buttons.
5. **Project 5: Ball Switch** - Detecting tilt and motion.
6. **Project 6: Active Buzzer** - Generating simple sounds.
7. **Project 7: Passive Buzzer** - Playing melodies.
8. **Project 8: Servo** - Controlling precise movement.
9. **Project 9: Analog Joystick Module** - 2-axis control.
10. **Project 10: Ultrasonic Sensor Module** - Measuring distance.
11. **Project 11: Relay Module** - Controlling high-voltage circuits.
12. **Project 12: IR Receiver Module** - Remote control integration.
13. **Project 13: Membrane Switch Module** - Keypad inputs.
14. **Project 14: LCD1602 I2C Module** - Displaying text.
15. **Project 15: Thermometer** - Measuring temperature with a thermistor.
16. **Project 16: Eight LED with 74HC595** - Shift register basics.
17. **Project 17: The Serial Monitor** - Advanced debugging.
18. **Project 18: Photocell** - Measuring light intensity.
19. **Project 19: 74HC595 And Segment Display** - Controlling 7-segment displays.
20. **Project 20: Four Digital Seven Segment Display** - Multi-digit display.
21. **Project 21: DC Motors** - Basic motor control.
22. **Project 22: Stepper Motor** - Precise rotation control.
23. **Project 23: Controlling Stepper Motor With Remote** - IR integration.
24. **Project 24: Water Level Detection Sensor Module** - Liquid sensing.
25. **Project 25: Sound Sensor Module** - Sound level detection.
